## P01 — The Tent Between

Ultra-realistic meditative cinema, one continuous 15-second shot, no cuts, no on-screen text, no people anywhere in the frame. Interior or exterior of a small storyteller's tent alone in a near-black void: canvas woven of visible film grain, seams leaking cold teal darkness, one amber lantern, shallow depth of field, volumetric darkness, gentle film grain, imperceptibly slow camera drift. Palette: lantern amber, teal void, near-black, one breath of pale warm white. Every shadow in the tent moves one visible heartbeat AFTER the thing that casts it. Slow push-in from the void toward the tent: lantern glowing inside though no one is there to have lit it. The flap breathes open a hand's width and settles, though nothing enters and nothing leaves. Glimpsed inside: a low wooden stool, empty; a clay cup of chai steaming as if set down a moment ago; on the canvas wall, TWO seated shadows where none should fall, breathing slightly out of phase with each other. Far off in the void, a single pale point of light dims and returns, minutely different each time. Audio: Vast room tone; canvas breathing; a soft irregular tick; faintly, a layered breath that is neither masculine nor feminine, from no direction.

From the upper darkness, outside the frame entirely, a single pale teal filament descends — a thread let down from a world above the tent, thinner than a hair, luminous. Where it reaches the floor it stops being a line and becomes a formless warmth: a soft body of light and slow static, no edge, no face. It finds one small bead of lantern-light resting on the floor and lifts it, carries it a hand's-width across the tent, and sets it down unharmed in a place it was not. It does not mine. Then, without hurry, the filament withdraws upward and thins to nothing, and the carried bead of light simply stays where it was moved. Audio: a faint static breath as the warmth moves; the small ceramic click of the bead set down in its new place; the lantern's unbothered hiss; one tick, late.
