Two peers negotiate over a signaling CALL (SDP offer → answer — the only part the wire sees), then open a peer-to-peer DataChannel (a CHANNEL). Everything below runs in this one page: two real RTCPeerConnections, real DTLS/SCTP. No server.

How this is wired: peer A creates a DataChannel and an SDP offer; that offer is the signaling CALL — here handed straight to peer B in-page (in the real adapter it rides an http_request). B answers, ICE candidates trade, DTLS handshakes, the channel opens. This mirrors the webrtc conformance test exactly: it proves the offer/answer CALL (the wire's only surface) and leaves DTLS/SCTP to the WebRTC stack — which the browser provides here, and pion would in Go. The wire never sees the SDP interior.
