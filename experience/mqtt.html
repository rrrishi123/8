A persistent broker connection. subscribe to a topic and messages arrive as frames (CHANNEL); publish fires one message into a topic (CALL). Topic routing and QoS are the "capability-shaped" bits the adapter owns — the wire never learns about topics.

How this is wired: mqtt.js (from a CDN) over WebSocket to a public test broker, so you get real pub/sub with zero setup. The topic is randomized per visit so you only see your own frames.
